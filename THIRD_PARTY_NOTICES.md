# Third-party notices

The renderers that draw what Mermaid's parser reads port parts of these
projects to TypeScript: Mermaid's subgraph and edge handling around dagre
(`src/dagre/`), dagre's layout pipeline (`src/dagre/dagre.ts`), and d3's time
intervals, tick steps and strftime formats for Gantt axes (`src/gantt/time.ts`).
Their notices follow.
